# 🐾 Mochi's Healthy Week
A virtual-pet game (Talking Tom style) about **UN SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being**.

**▶ Play:** enable GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root) and open the link, or just open `index.html` in any browser. No install needed.

## Story
Mochi has just moved to your village and no one taught Mochi how to stay healthy. You are Mochi's buddy. Keep Mochi healthy for **7 days** (45 s each) to become a *Healthy Hero*. If health drops to 15% Mochi must go to the doctor.

## How to play
| Action | What it does | SDG link |
|---|---|---|
| 🍎 Feed | Choose healthy food, water or junk. Too many treats gives a tummy ache | 3.4 non-communicable diseases, 3.9 safe water |
| 🧼 Wash | Tap Scrub 10 times to wash hands | 3.3 preventing infectious disease |
| 🏃 Exercise | Tap-fast 6-second mini-game | 3.4 physical and mental health |
| 💬 Talk | Mochi shares a worry, pick the kind reply | 3.4 mental health and well-being |
| 😴 Sleep | Restores energy | 3.4 well-being |
| 🔊 Say something | Mochi repeats your text in a funny voice | Fun (Talking Tom feel) |

Five stats (Food, Energy, Clean, Fitness, Mood) decay every second. **Health = average of all five.** Every action shows a real health fact.
